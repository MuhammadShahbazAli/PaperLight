Paperlight PDF Reader: Privacy Policy

Effective date: [13/11/2026]

Paperlight PDF Reader ("the app") is made by Storefront Architects. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your device.

The app has no internet access

The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything over the internet. Android enforces this, and anyone can confirm it on the app's Google Play page under "Permissions".

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only, in its private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Your library: the name, size, dates and location of each PDF you add, and the folders you add. The app stores where a document is, not a copy of it.
  • Reading data: the page you reached in each document, your bookmarks, your favorites and your list of recently opened documents.
  • Settings: your choices for theme, page colors, reading mode, zoom and so on.
  • Previews: small images of the first page of each document, so the library shows thumbnails.
  • Temporary copies: a few storage providers only allow a document to be read as a stream. The app then keeps a temporary private copy while the document is open, and deletes it afterwards, or the next time the app starts.

Passwords for protected PDFs are passed straight to Android's PDF renderer to open the document. They are never stored or recorded.

None of this is backed up to the cloud or copied to a new phone. The app opts out of both Android backup and device-to-device transfer.

Access to your files

The app doesn't use storage permissions. It can only open the documents and folders you choose in Android's file picker, or that another app hands it ("Open with"). You can withdraw access at any time by removing a document or folder from the library.

When you rename or delete a file, the app does that to the file itself, only after you confirm it. "Remove from library" never deletes the file.

Sharing

When you use Share, Android lets you choose the app to send the document to. That app receives the document under its own privacy policy. Paperlight sends nothing anywhere by itself.

Deleting your data

  • Recent > Clear history empties the recent list.
  • Settings > Storage > Clear cached thumbnails deletes the previews.
  • Removing a document from the library deletes its reading data.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything the app stored. Your PDF files themselves are not affected.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated first and its effective date changed. Any change that sends information off your device would also need the internet permission, which Google Play would show.
